Free Credential Exposure Check

Send your company domain and I'll run a one-time check for your team's leaked passwords and stolen sessions — free, no obligation. See your current exposure before deciding anything.

The fastest way to know whether credential monitoring is worth it is to look at what’s already out there. So before you commit to anything, I’ll run a one-time exposure check on your domain, free.

What you get#

Send me your company domain and I’ll check it against both sources — infostealer logs (live, service-specific credentials copied off infected machines) and breach data (leaked logins from other companies’ breaches). Then I send you a short, plain-language summary:

  • How many exposed credentials are tied to your domain
  • Which are live / usable versus old breach noise
  • Which accounts and services are affected
  • My read on how exposed you actually are, and what to do first

No dashboard to learn, no raw dump to wade through — just the findings and what they mean. If you want to see the exact format first, here’s a sample report.

What it costs, and what it commits you to#

Nothing, and nothing. It’s a free one-time check with no obligation. You see your current exposure, and then you decide whether ongoing monitoring is worth it. Plenty of checks come back quiet — that’s a perfectly good outcome to buy peace of mind with.

A note on privacy#

I only check the domain you give me, and only for credentials that have already leaked publicly — nothing is accessed, logged into, or touched. Any live credentials I find are shown to you so you can kill them; they are never used. If we go further than the free check, I act as your data processor under a proper agreement — the FAQ covers the GDPR specifics.
