The fastest way to know whether credential monitoring is worth it is to look at what's already out there. So before you commit to anything, I'll run a **one-time exposure check on your domain, free.**

## What you get

Send me your company domain and I'll check it against both sources — **infostealer logs** (live, service-specific credentials copied off infected machines) and **breach data** (leaked logins from other companies' breaches). Then I send you a short, plain-language summary:

- How many exposed credentials are tied to your domain
- Which are **live / usable** versus old breach noise
- Which accounts and services are affected
- My read on how exposed you actually are, and what to do first

No dashboard to learn, no raw dump to wade through — just the findings and what they mean. If you want to see the exact format first, here's a [sample report](/monitoring/sample-report/).

Nothing, and nothing. It's a free one-time check with **no obligation**. You see your current exposure, and *then* you decide whether ongoing [monitoring](/monitoring/) is worth it. Plenty of checks come back quiet — that's a perfectly good outcome to buy peace of mind with.
